Every protocol on this board records no token in the snapshot — no market cap, no listing, no symbol. The radar asks what such a protocol would be worth if it did carry one: it takes the protocol's TVL, multiplies it by the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of tokenized protocols in the same category, then adjusts for recent growth and for age. The result is an implied value, not a price and not a promise — a way to sort a crowded field before doing the deeper work.

How the model works

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier, rounded to the nearest dollar (and never below $1). The peer multiple is the category's median market-cap-to-TVL ratio among protocols whose token already trades. Growth adjusts for the 7-day TVL change, clamped between −60% and +120% and counted at half weight. Maturity starts at 0.6 for a brand-new protocol and rises to 1.0 at three years old; a protocol of unknown age counts as 365 days.

Peer ratios are winsorized at 10×, shrunk toward the global median (0.2227) with prior weight 4, and clamped to [0.02, 3.0]. A category with no tokenized peers uses the global median outright.
